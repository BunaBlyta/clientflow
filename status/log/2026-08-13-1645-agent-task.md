# Mobile visual polish

## What changed

- Made the bottom tab footer a darker, theme-aware surface with a clean top
  edge and restrained shadow so its labels remain readable.
- Replaced pill-shaped status badges with compact rounded labels.
- Removed harsh borders from secondary, cancel, logout, and language controls;
  used subtle surface contrast and small shadows instead.
- Added a restrained accent shadow to primary buttons without changing their
  actions or dimensions.
